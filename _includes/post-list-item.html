{%- comment -%} One post row for the home and blog list pages. Both used to carry their own copy of this markup; a single include means adding thumbnail/tag support only had to happen once, and the two lists can't drift out of sync the way the post/nav markup did before. Usage: {%- include post-list-item.html post=post -%} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign post = include.post -%}
